Meet Dr. Sofia Mendel, DDS

Dr. Mendel has been creating healthy, confident smiles in Austin, Texas for over a decade. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio and completed her undergraduate studies in Biology at Texas A&M University.

Bright Smile Dental offers preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, and Invisalign. Dr. Mendel is a member of the American Dental Association and the Texas Dental Association.
