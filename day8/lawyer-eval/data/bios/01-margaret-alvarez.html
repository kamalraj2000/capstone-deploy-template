Margaret Alvarez

Partner, New York

m.alvarez@whitfieldgrant.example.com · +1 (212) 555-0142

Margaret Alvarez is a partner in Whitfield & Grant's New York office and co-chair of the firm's Private Equity practice. She advises strategic acquirers and private equity sponsors on complex domestic and cross-border transactions across the Corporate, Mergers & Acquisitions, and Private Equity practices.

Representative matters

  • Advised a private equity sponsor on the take-private of a NASDAQ-listed software company.
