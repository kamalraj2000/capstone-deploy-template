Daniel Okafor

Senior Associate — Chicago

When businesses find themselves in high-stakes disputes, they call Daniel. A tenacious advocate with a knack for making complex facts simple, Daniel has tried cases and argued motions across a broad range of commercial litigation and product liability matters. Clients praise his responsiveness, his creativity, and his refusal to be outworked.

Daniel serves as the pro bono coordinator for Brightline's Chicago office, where he has built partnerships with neighborhood legal-aid clinics.

Daniel earned his J.D. from Loyola University Chicago School of Law and his B.A. in Psychology from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. He is admitted to practice in Illinois and before the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois.
